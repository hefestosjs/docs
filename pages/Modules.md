### Modules

Using the `bun module` command, you can generate one or multiple modules simultaneously. The available modules are:

- [**Authentication**](/docs/pages/Authentication)
- [**Mailer**](/docs/pages/Mailer)
- [**Upload**](/docs/pages/Upload)
- [**Views**](/docs/pages/Views)
